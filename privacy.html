Privacy Policy

OutWatch for iPhone and Apple Watch · Effective November 20, 2026

OutWatch collects, uploads and shares zero personal data.

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No OutWatch servers or cloud storage. The app never sends your data anywhere.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no third-party code at all.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

Everything OutWatch needs to work, including your settings, home base, schedule and check-in history, stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains each kind of data the app uses on your device, when it uses it, and why. In App Store Connect, OutWatch's App Privacy label is Data Not Collected.

1. Location

OutWatch is a departure alarm, so it uses your location for one thing: checking whether you've physically left your home blast radius or arrived at your target checkpoint, so the alarm can turn off.

  • When In Use: to show your position and lock your home base or checkpoints on the map, and to measure your live distance during an alarm.
  • Always (background): only so a running alarm can keep checking your departure, and turn itself off, while your phone is locked in your pocket as you walk, cycle or drive away.
  • On demand only: location runs only (a) during an active alarm session, from the 10-minute heads-up until the alarm is turned off or its time limit runs out, (b) while the geofence map is on screen, or (c) briefly, to get a first fix when you set your home base. OutWatch never tracks your location in standby.
  • Location is processed on your device and never uploaded, logged to a server or shared. The only coordinates saved are your home base and checkpoints, which you choose yourself.
  • The map is drawn by Apple's MapKit. Map tiles are loaded from Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy. OutWatch doesn't send your searches or position to anyone.

2. Motion & Fitness

  • Stationary drift guard: during an alarm, OutWatch checks your on-device motion activity (for example "stationary"). If indoor GPS or Wi-Fi positioning seems to move a phone that is lying still, it's held to a much stricter standard, so the alarm can't be fooled while you stay in bed.
  • Offline stride fallback: step count and stride distance from the motion coprocessor are used only when GPS can't get a fix, for example in a stairwell during the 60-second mute.
  • Motion data is read only during an alarm session, processed on your device, and never stored externally or shared.

3. HealthKit (Apple Health)

OutWatch uses HealthKit only for Rainy Day Indoor Redemption, an optional mode for bad weather where you can turn off the alarm by keeping your heart rate up indoors instead of going outside.

  • Read: live heart rate, read strictly on-device and only while a Rainy Day Indoor Redemption session is active. Reading stops when the session ends.
  • Write (workouts): watchOS requires workout permission for an app to run the temporary indoor workout session that keeps the heart-rate sensor sampling continuously. When the session finishes, OutWatch immediately discards that workout. It is never saved, so it doesn't clutter your Health history or Activity rings. (Heart-rate readings that your Apple Watch takes on its own are managed by watchOS, as usual.)
  • HealthKit data is never stored outside your device, never uploaded, never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never shared with or sold to third parties. It isn't stored in iCloud by OutWatch either.
  • The only heart-rate values OutWatch keeps are the current reading during the session and the session's peak BPM, saved in your on-device check-in history. They're shared only with your own paired iPhone or Apple Watch, so both screens show the same progress.

4. System alarms & notifications

  • AlarmKit (iOS 26 and later): your departure alarm is scheduled with the system alarm service, so it rings even when OutWatch is closed.
  • Local notifications: departure cues and reminders are scheduled on your device by your device. There are no push notifications and no notification servers.

5. Apple Watch sync (WatchConnectivity)

Your iPhone and Apple Watch keep your settings, the alarm's live state (stage, distance to your goal, steps, heart rate in Rainy Day mode) and your check-in history in sync. They use Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, which sends data directly between your own paired devices. OutWatch has no server in between and can't see this data.

6. On-device storage (UserDefaults)

Your settings, schedule, home base and checkpoint coordinates, language preference and check-in history (date, unlock method, duration, distance, and steps or peak heart rate) are saved in the app's local storage on each device. This storage is used only by OutWatch itself, as declared in the app's privacy manifest. Deleting the app deletes this data.

8. Children

OutWatch doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If OutWatch ever started collecting data, it would only happen with clear notice in the app and an updated App Store privacy label first.

隐私政策

OutWatch（iPhone 与 Apple Watch）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

OutWatch 不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人数据。

  • 无需注册账户，无需登录。
  • 没有 OutWatch 服务器，也没有云存储，App 不会把您的数据发送到任何地方。
  • 不含任何分析、崩溃上报、广告或追踪 SDK，也不含任何第三方代码。
  • 不会跨 App 或网站追踪您，也绝不出售或共享任何数据。

OutWatch 运行所需的一切数据，包括设置、家的位置、日程和打卡记录，都只保存在您的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上。本政策逐项说明 App 在您设备上使用了哪些数据、何时使用以及原因。OutWatch 在 App Store Connect 中的“App 隐私”标签为“未收集数据”。

1. 位置

OutWatch 是一款出门闹钟，使用位置只为一件事：确认您已离开家的警戒半径或到达目标检查点，以便关闭闹钟。

  • 使用 App 期间：在地图上显示您的位置、锁定家或检查点，并在闹钟期间测量实时距离。
  • 始终（后台）：仅用于让正在响的闹钟在您锁屏并把手机放进口袋、步行、骑行或开车离开时，仍能继续确认您是否已出门，并自动关闭。
  • 仅按需使用：位置只在以下情况下运行：(a) 闹钟进行中，从提前 10 分钟的提醒开始，直到闹钟关闭或时间用完；(b) 地理围栏地图显示在屏幕上时；(c) 设定家的位置时，为获取第一次定位而短暂开启。待机时 OutWatch 绝不追踪您的位置。
  • 位置只在您的设备上处理，不会上传、不会记录到服务器，也不会共享。唯一会保存的坐标是您自己选定的家和检查点。
  • 地图由 Apple 的 MapKit 绘制，地图图块从 Apple 地图加载，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。OutWatch 不会把您的搜索内容或位置发送给任何人。

2. 运动与健身

  • 静止漂移防护：闹钟期间，OutWatch 会查看设备上的运动状态（例如“静止”）。如果室内 GPS 或 Wi-Fi 定位让一部静止的手机看起来在移动，App 会采用严格得多的判定标准，因此您躺在床上时无法骗过闹钟。
  • 离线步幅备用：运动协处理器提供的步数和步幅距离，只在 GPS 无法定位时使用，例如在 60 秒静音期间走楼梯。
  • 运动数据只在闹钟期间读取，在设备上处理，绝不存储到外部，也不会共享。

3. HealthKit（Apple 健康）

OutWatch 只在“雨天室内补救”模式下使用 HealthKit。这是一个可选模式：遇到恶劣天气时，您可以在室内保持较高心率来关闭闹钟，而不必出门。

  • 读取：实时心率，严格在设备上读取，且仅在“雨天室内补救”进行期间读取。模式结束后立即停止读取。
  • 写入（体能训练）：watchOS 要求 App 获得体能训练权限，才能运行让心率传感器持续采样的临时室内训练。训练结束后，OutWatch 会立即丢弃这次训练，绝不保存，因此不会打乱您的健康记录或活动圆环。（Apple Watch 自行测量的心率数据仍由 watchOS 照常管理。）
  • HealthKit 数据绝不存储到设备之外、绝不上传、绝不用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也绝不与第三方共享或出售。OutWatch 也不会把这些数据存到 iCloud。
  • OutWatch 只保留模式进行中的当前心率，以及本次的最高心率（保存在设备上的打卡记录中）。这些数值只会同步到您自己配对的 iPhone 或 Apple Watch，以便两块屏幕显示相同的进度。

4. 系统闹钟与通知

  • AlarmKit（iOS 26 及更高版本）：出门闹钟通过系统闹钟服务安排，即使 OutWatch 已关闭也会响铃。
  • 本地通知：出门提醒由您的设备在本机安排。没有推送通知，也没有通知服务器。

5. Apple Watch 同步（WatchConnectivity）

您的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 会同步设置、闹钟的实时状态（阶段、到目标的距离、步数，以及“雨天”模式下的心率）和打卡记录。同步使用 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 框架，数据直接在您自己配对的设备之间传输。中间没有 OutWatch 服务器，OutWatch 也看不到这些数据。

6. 本地存储（UserDefaults）

您的设置、日程、家与检查点的坐标、语言偏好和打卡记录（日期、解除方式、用时、距离，以及步数或最高心率）保存在每台设备上 App 自己的本地存储中。这些存储只供 OutWatch 自己使用，已在 App 的隐私清单中声明。删除 App 即会删除这些数据。

7. 权限一览

权限用途使用时机
位置（使用期间）地图、家的位置、实时距离地图显示时；闹钟期间

8. 儿童

OutWatch 不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

9. 政策变更

如果本政策有变，更新后的版本会发布在本页并标明新的生效日期。若 OutWatch 将来要收集任何数据，一定会先在 App 内明确告知，并更新 App Store 隐私标签。
