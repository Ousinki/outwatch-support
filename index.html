iPhone + Apple Watch

The alarm that only stops once you're out the door.

OutWatch is a departure alarm. Set the time you need to leave, and when it goes off it keeps ringing until your iPhone or Apple Watch confirms you've actually left home or reached your chosen checkpoint. Walk, run, cycle, catch a taxi or drive: any way out counts.

How it works

Each departure builds up in three stages, then has one goal: get outside.

T−10 min · Heads-upA gentle cue to wrap up and put your shoes on.
T−3 min · Final callA more urgent cue to head for the door.
T−0 · LockdownThe alarm sounds until you've left, or until the departure time limit runs out.
Unlock mode

Blast Radius Escape

Pin your home on the map and choose a radius (80–400 m, 150 m by default). The alarm turns off once you're confirmed outside that circle.

Unlock mode

Target Checkpoint

Prefer a destination? Pick a checkpoint, like a park, a bus stop or the gym, with a 30–150 m capture radius. The alarm turns off when you arrive.

During lockdown

60-Second Lace-Up Mute

Need a moment for shoes or the elevator? Mute the alarm for 60 seconds (up to 3 times per alarm). If you haven't moved toward your goal when the minute is up, the alarm comes back louder. Muting isn't available in the last 3 minutes.

Bad weather

Rainy Day Indoor Redemption

Storm outside? Switch to indoor mode and keep your heart rate at or above your target (125 BPM by default) for a set time (3 minutes by default), measured live by Apple Watch. If your heart rate doesn't rise, the alarm goes back to outdoor mode.

Two devices, one alarm

iPhone + Apple Watch

Settings and alarm progress stay in sync between your iPhone and Apple Watch. The alarm can ring on both or just one, and you can complete it on either. If you leave with only your watch, the phone at home stands down.

Fair, not foolable

Drift-proof

OutWatch waits for several accurate location fixes in a row before it counts you as outside. That way a phone sitting on the nightstand can't turn the alarm off just because indoor GPS or Wi-Fi positioning wanders.

Quick start

Setup takes about two minutes.

  1. Allow permissions.

    OutWatch needs location to check that you've left, and notifications or system alarms so it can ring when the app is closed. Choose Always for location so the alarm can still turn off while your phone is locked in your pocket. Motion & Fitness and Health are optional; they power the drift check and Rainy Day mode.

  2. Set your home base.

    On the map, tap Lock Current GPS as Home while you're at home, then adjust the blast radius. Or switch to Target Checkpoint and pick a destination.

  3. Add departure times.

    Add one or more times, choose the days of the week, and pick a departure time limit (5–30 minutes). Leave the schedule on.

  4. Open OutWatch on your Apple Watch once.

    This syncs your settings to the watch and lets it arm its wrist alarm for your next departure. Keep the watch on and charged overnight if you want it to wake you.

  5. Leave.

    When the alarm goes off, head outside. Once you're past the line, it turns off by itself and your streak goes up.

FAQ

Common questions about how OutWatch behaves.

Does it matter how I leave: walking, cycling, driving?

No. OutWatch only checks where you are, not how you got there. Walking, running, cycling, an e-bike, a taxi or bus, or driving out of an underground garage all count once you're confirmed outside the blast radius or inside your checkpoint.

Why hasn't the alarm turned off yet? I'm already outside.

OutWatch waits for a few accurate GPS fixes in a row, clearly past the line. That usually takes a few seconds after you cross it. In a garage, an underpass or between tall buildings, it may take a little longer for a clear signal.

Check that Location is set to Always with Precise Location on (Settings › OutWatch › Location), and that your home base was locked at home rather than somewhere else.

Could indoor GPS drift turn the alarm off while I'm still in bed?

That's exactly what OutWatch guards against. It ignores coarse Wi-Fi and cell positions and rejects single jumps. It requires a run of accurate fixes outside the line, and any fix that clearly places you at home resets that run. If Motion & Fitness reports the phone lying perfectly still, the bar is higher still.

What happens if I don't leave in time?

When the departure time limit (5–30 minutes, chosen in Settings) runs out, the alarm stops and that departure is recorded as missed. You can see your check-ins and missed departures in the history.

How does the 60-Second Lace-Up Mute work?

During lockdown, tap Mute to silence the alarm for 60 seconds while you get ready. You can do this up to 3 times per alarm, but not in the last 3 minutes before the time limit. When the minute ends, OutWatch checks whether you've made about 12 m of progress toward your goal. If GPS can't get a fix (in a stairwell or elevator, say), your steps count instead. If you haven't moved, the alarm comes back louder.

How does Rainy Day Indoor Redemption work?

When it's not safe to go outside, switch to indoor mode from the lockdown screen. Exercise in place, and once your Apple Watch has measured your heart rate at or above your target for long enough, the alarm turns off.

You can enter indoor mode up to 2 times per alarm. If your heart rate doesn't rise for 2 minutes, the alarm goes back to outdoor mode. Heart rate is read live and only during this mode, and OutWatch doesn't save the session to your Health history (see Privacy).

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Everything except Rainy Day heart-rate mode works on iPhone alone. With a watch, the alarm can also ring on your wrist, and you can leave with only the watch: OutWatch notices it moving away from the phone at home and turns off the phone's alarm.

My Apple Watch didn't ring. What should I check?

Open OutWatch on the watch at least once after changing your schedule so it can arm for the next departure. Make sure the watch is on your wrist and charged, and that it isn't in Sleep Focus or Low Power Mode, which can hold back alerts.

Check where alerts are sent (Both, Phone Only or Watch Only) in OutWatch's sound and haptics settings, and keep the watch paired and within Bluetooth or Wi-Fi range of your iPhone so they stay in sync.

Will the alarm ring if I force-quit the app?

Yes. On iOS 26 and later, OutWatch schedules its departure alarm with the system's alarm service (AlarmKit), so it rings even when the app is closed or force-quit. On earlier iOS versions it uses time-sensitive local notifications. Allow notifications and, on iOS 26, alarms.

Does OutWatch drain my battery?

Only while an alarm is running. GPS turns on for the departure window (from the 10-minute heads-up until you leave or the time limit runs out) and while you're setting your home or checkpoint on the map. The rest of the time OutWatch doesn't track your location at all.

Does OutWatch collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, servers, analytics or ads. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Read the full privacy policy.
